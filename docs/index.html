What it does

Everything you need to keep on top of the month, nothing you don't.

Bills checklist

Add each bill with its due day and amount, and tick it off once it's paid. Overdue bills are highlighted, and you can sort by name, due date or amount.

Resets on pay day

Choose your pay day (1st to 31st). When a new month starts on that day, all bills go back to unpaid, ready for the next cycle.

Money owed & balance

Track money people owe you, and see your balance, what's still to pay, what's left, and your expected balance once you've been paid back.

Questions

How do I install an APK?

Tap Download APK on your phone, then open the downloaded file. The first time, Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser or Files app. Allow it, then tap Install. A warning that the app isn't from the Play Store is expected for apps installed this way.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone, in the app's own storage. There's no account, no server and no connection to your bank. You type your figures in yourself.

How do I back up my data or move it to a new phone?

Open the menu and tap Export Dataset, then save the file to Google Drive, email it to yourself, or store it in Files. On the new phone, use Import Dataset to load it back. Export Bills CSV gives you a spreadsheet of your bills.

Does money owed reset on pay day too?

No. Only bills reset automatically. Money owed stays as it is until you tick it off or use Reset Money Owed in the menu.

What if my pay day is the 31st?

In shorter months the cycle starts on the last day of the month instead, e.g. 28 February or 30 April.
